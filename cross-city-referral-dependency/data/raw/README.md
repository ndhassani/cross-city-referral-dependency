Put the five quarterly Excel files of *Referral of Medical Cases* here (they are not stored on GitHub):

```
Referral of Medical Cases 2024 Q4 .xlsx
Referral of Medical Cases 2025 Q1 .xlsx
Referral of Medical Cases 2025 Q2 .xlsx
Referral of Medical Cases 2025 Q3 .xlsx
Referral of Medical Cases 2025 Q4 .xlsx
```

The quarter is read from the file name (four-digit year, then `Q` and the quarter number), so other file names work as long as they contain e.g. `2025 Q1`.
